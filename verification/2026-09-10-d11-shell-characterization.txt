DESIGNED NEVER TO FIRE — WHY THIS CANNOT CLOSE A GOAL
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Each rule's single premise is a capability term. Discharging it would
require a fact or rule conclusion with that head. Gate S6 proves
mechanically that probing each capability term as a goal against ALL
shipped packs yields zero candidate rules; no library axiom carries
either head; a library-only cold start (taught rules 0, axioms 0) has no
other fact source. Therefore both rules can produce candidate partial
matches and concrete unmatched-premise residuals ONLY — a matched goal
stays unclosed. The transcripts show FAILED on every after-state probe.
