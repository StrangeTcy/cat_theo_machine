  FIRST entry carrying a goal (head goal short-circuits the
  recursion). The attempt cycle therefore "proves" the initial
  obligation, not the conclusion. Confirmed by direct extraction on
  both rehearsal records and on E2 (Action 4). Any conversion judged
  by the attempt cycle today judges the wrong obligation.

R3 — VACUOUS-SUCCESS TRAP (HIGH).
  With R2, when the first obligation's goal restates meaning-structure
  knowledge (natural for "initial" obligations), attempt_training_
  record reports SUCCESS and retained=True while never touching the
  real conclusion — observed for BOTH rehearsal records. The success
  path's method text is hardcoded "invariance pipeline (strategy hint
  Invariance)" regardless of the actual hint (Extremal and Pigeonhole
  hints both produced it). The join rule's "genuine partial match"
  gate is load-bearing: per-obligation checking must replace
  conclusion-only judgment, and R2 must be fixed, before any converted
  record's SUCCESS is meaningful.
