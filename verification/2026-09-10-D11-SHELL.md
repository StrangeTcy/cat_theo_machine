The D11-SHELL-ENG repair-wave ticket (Agent 2). Phase 1 (this lane,
ratified at `b55d66e`) established the frontier: bare `eq` matches, the
`nosolutions` and `forall`/`implies` shells are silent, and the blocker
for sound closure is the checker's missing binder discipline
(`planner.py:189`). This is the characterization half: the machine must
NAME what it lacks on those shells. It is not proof support.

## The two rules

```text
nosolutions_introduction_characterization
  premises     NeedContradictionFromArbitrarySolution(?d ?u ?q)
  conclusion   nosolutions(?d ?u ?q)

forall_implies_decomposition_characterization
  premises     NeedBinderSafeImplication(?n ?p ?q)
  conclusion   forall(?n implies(?p ?q))
```

Reading (per the repair-wave brief): the nosolutions capability is a
checked contradiction from an arbitrary assumed in-domain solution; the
forall/implies capability is to assume the antecedent in a scoped
context, derive the consequent, and discharge the assumption — never to
prove the antecedent itself.

## Designed never to fire

Each rule's single premise is a capability term. No shipped pack rule
concludes either capability head, no library axiom carries either head,
and no fact of a library-only cold start can produce them, so the
premises are undischargeable by the generic rewrite calculus. The gate
proves it mechanically (S6): probing each capability term as a goal
finds zero candidate rules anywhere in the shipped packs. The rules
therefore produce candidate partial matches and concrete residuals only;
a matched goal stays unclosed. Promoting either rule to a
proof-producing rule requires the binder/contradiction checker
discipline the substrate does not have (`planner.py:189`) and is out of
scope by construction.

## Vocabulary provenance (inspected before adding)

Existing: `ForAllLabel`, `ContradictionLabel`, `ExistsLabel`, `NotLabel`
(the logical family, `labels.py`), plus `NeedLabel` (dependency
requests) and `FreshenedLabel`/`BinderLabel` (the planner's side
conditions). `ForAllLabel` is reused here with its exact denotation, the
way D11-PORT reused `ExprEqLabel` for `eq`. New: `ImpliesLabel` and
`NoSolutionsLabel` (the missing members of the logical family's surface
spelling) and the two `Need*` capability labels — no existing label
denotes them. `NeedLabel` itself was NOT reused for the capability
premises: it is the dependency-request term, and overloading it would
make a capability residual indistinguishable from a theorem request.

## Explicitly not claimed

No quantified proof support. No forall-introduction, implication-
introduction, or refutation checking. The rules expose child obligations
as concrete unmatched premises; sound closure of the shells is
LOGIC-CHECKER-ENG's ticket (Agent 3) and must not ride through these
rules. A mapped head is not shell support: only the checker-validated
calculus would be.
