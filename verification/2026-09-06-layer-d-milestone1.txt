      rule order), with the reason recorded in-code: the numeric fields
      (beam/alpha/beta/strength) are placeholder atoms after decode and never

      the live baseline. Mismatched snapshots are still refused on start,
      goal, mode, and rule order.

      rule-order fields differ (InsertionOrder vs GoalHeadOrder), i.e. the
      fixture wrote a snapshot for a different problem than it asked about.
      Now the fixture checkpoints probe._heuristic_for_mode(M.BFSLabel).
