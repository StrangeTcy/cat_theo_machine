**Defect 1 — pre-existing on the INT line; the worker-transport receipt
contract is unmeetable on this base.** In `proof.py::_prove` (candidate lines;
the file is byte-identical from preflight `ddc3d79` through the INT tip, so
the defect was live when the 295/6/4 baseline was measured — the preflight
shard logs contain zero `benchmarking all current search modes` lines):

- line 3826: `if IsKnowledge(self.goal)() is M.truth_value:` — its body
  (direct search + `return` on every path, lines 3827–3851) is intact;
- lines 3853–3947: the cache→comparison-benchmark→recommended-search block,
  which on the lane's base (`41e8078`, lines 3706+) sits **dedented at 12
  spaces, outside** that `if`, is nested **at 16 spaces, inside** it — after
  unconditional returns, i.e. dead code;
- line 3948: `else:` with `research mode - skip stored search-comparison
  shortcuts` pairs with `IsKnowledge`, not with any research-mode condition.

Net effect: every non-knowledge concrete proof skips the derivation-cache
lookup, the `CompareSearchModes` benchmark (the only spawner of worker
subprocesses from `prove`), and the recommended search, and falls through to
in-process mixed search. The foreground submissions in §4 therefore produce
zero `search_compare/run-*` directories, and the receipts step fails with
`FileNotFoundError: …/search_compare` — the lane's 15/15 receipt group cannot
pass. Repair (dedent the block, dispose of the stranded `else`) re-enables
worker fan-out, cache hits, and comparison evidence for **every** proof on the
line — a line-wide behavior and timing change that re-baselines the suite. It
belongs to the owning lane plus the operator, not to INT unilaterally. No
workaround was applied: routing the foreground path around `prove` to
manufacture receipts would be fabricating evidence, not integrating.
