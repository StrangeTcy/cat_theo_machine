B-4  Checker binder / contradiction discipline — THE PROOF-CRITICAL GAP.
  Evidence: planner.py records that the caller owns freshness because the
  substrate has no binder discipline; no connective-specific checker semantics
  exist for forall, implication, or no-solutions-by-contradiction.
  Fix: checker-native, with enforced semantics rather than names —
    ForallIntroduction(variable, body, freshness_evidence)
    ImpliesIntroduction(assumption, derivation)
    ContradictionIntroduction(assumption, contradiction)
    NoSolutionsIntroduction(domain, unknowns, assumed_solution, contradiction)
  Required properties: eigenvariable freshness enforced at check time and not
  free in undischarged assumptions; assumptions scoped with no sibling leakage;
  capture-safe substitution; replay rejects a certificate missing freshness or
  scope evidence; generic rewrite rules cannot close these shells without the
  appropriate checker rule. Implication introduction means assume the antecedent
  in a scoped context, derive the consequent, discharge — it must NOT be
  implemented as proving the antecedent.
  This is the gap between A1 (naming the residual) and A2 (closing the proof).
  Owner: INT / logic lane. Status: not started on any branch.
